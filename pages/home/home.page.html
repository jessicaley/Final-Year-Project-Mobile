Professional Conduct

  • Treat colleagues with respect at all times
  • Maintain professional communication
  • Dress appropriately for the workplace
  • Avoid disruptive behavior
  • Resolve conflicts professionally

Data Security

  • Protect confidential information
  • Follow security protocols
  • Never share login credentials
  • Report security concerns
  • Secure workstations when away

Attendance

  • Arrive on time for work
  • Notify manager if late/absent
  • Follow time-off procedures
  • Limit breaks appropriately
  • Accurately record hours

Technology Use

  • Use equipment for business purposes
  • Install approved software only
  • Limit personal internet use
  • Maintain professional communication
  • Back up important files

Health & Safety

  • Follow safety procedures
  • Report accidents immediately
  • Maintain clean workspace
  • Use ergonomic best practices
  • Complete safety training

Compliance

  • Adhere to company policies
  • Avoid conflicts of interest
  • Never accept inappropriate gifts
  • Maintain accurate records
  • Report unethical behavior

These rules help create a secure, productive, and positive work environment for everyone at Vault Vision.

All team members are expected to understand and comply with these guidelines to maintain our company's standards of excellence.
